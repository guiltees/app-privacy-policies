# Privacy Policy for TimeShelf

**Last updated: October 3, 2026**

TimeShelf is a time tracking and countdown application designed to help users keep track of time since past events and time remaining until future events.

## Information We Collect

TimeShelf does not require users to create an account or sign in.

The app does not collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, contacts, or precise location.

Information that you enter into TimeShelf, including event names, dates, times, notes, categories, history, and preferences, is stored locally on your device.

## Data Sharing

TimeShelf does not sell, rent, or share your personal data with advertisers or other third parties.

## Backup and Restore

TimeShelf provides backup and restore functionality.

When you create or restore a backup, you choose how and where the backup file is stored or accessed. TimeShelf does not operate a server that receives or stores your backup data.

## Notifications and Reminders

TimeShelf may request permission to send notifications in order to provide event reminders and countdown-related notifications.

You can manage notification permissions at any time through your device settings.

## Third-Party and Device Services

TimeShelf may use standard Android system features or services to perform functions such as notifications and selecting, storing, or sharing backup files.

If you choose to use a third-party service for storing or sharing a backup file, that service may process the file according to its own privacy policy.

## Data Retention and Deletion

Data created within TimeShelf remains on your device until you edit or delete it, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app.

Backup copies created by you remain in the location you selected until you delete them.

## Children's Privacy

TimeShelf is not specifically directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if TimeShelf's features or data practices change.

Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
